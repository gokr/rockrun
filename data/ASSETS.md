# Asset Sources

## Graphics

### Procedural textures (Rockrun project, CC0)

Generated by `tools/genassets.py` (deterministic, seeded). Released into
the public domain under CC0 1.0.

- `texture/boulder_small.png`, `texture/boulder.png`, `texture/boulder_big.png`
  — spherical rocky boulders in three sizes
- `texture/diamond.png` — faceted teal crystal with glow
- `texture/dirt.png` — organic sand block texture

### Kenney Sokoban

Source: [Kenney Sokoban](https://kenney.nl/assets/sokoban), licensed under
[Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
(full text in `texture/LICENSE-KENNEY-SOKOBAN.txt`).

- `texture/wall.png`
- `texture/exit.png`

### Kenney Pixel Platformer

Source: [Kenney Pixel Platformer](https://kenney.nl/assets/pixel-platformer),
licensed under [Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
(full text in `texture/LICENSE-KENNEY-PIXEL-PLATFORMER.txt`).

- `texture/idle.png`, `texture/run.png`, `texture/dig.png` — animation
  strips (4/6/3 frames) cut from the adventurer character sheet of
  Kenney's Pixel Platformer; source sheet kept as `tools/kenney-characters.png`

## Sound effects

- [Kenney Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds):
  `sound/dig.ogg`, `sound/push.ogg`, `sound/land.ogg`
  (also used for the gem clink)
- [Kenney Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds):
  `sound/collect.ogg`, `sound/win.ogg`, `sound/lose.ogg`

Both packs are CC0 1.0; license texts in `sound/LICENSE-KENNEY-IMPACT.txt`
and `sound/LICENSE-KENNEY-INTERFACE.txt`.
